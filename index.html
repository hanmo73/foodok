About the data

Live alerts come directly from official government sources: the U.S. FDA (openFDA), UK Food Standards Agency, Canada CFIA, EU RASFF and the Hong Kong Centre for Food Safety. China GACC data comes from the monthly lists of foods refused entry published by China Customs, translated by Food OK. Other agencies are linked to their official portals while direct feeds are being connected. Dates shown are the agency’s report dates.
